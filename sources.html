Official sources & verification scope

Every link below is to an NBA, GitHub, Netherlands Gambling Authority (Ksa), NIST or scikit-learn primary/maintainer page. A source link verifies only the statement named in its row; it is not permission to reuse data and does not establish a betting edge.

Evidence boundary

No player/team numbers, odds, schedule rows, injury rows, ROI or correlation estimates are reproduced. NBA’s own terms limit NBA Statistics use in gambling; a public glossary or citation is not a data licence.
