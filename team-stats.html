What is refused and why

The brief’s “re-cut the model inputs” step is recorded as REFUSED, not silently performed. NBA.com Terms of Use sections 1 and 9(ii) restrict statistics in connection with gambling activity; until the owner decision (IR-T4) and rights gate G0 clear, engine/contracts.require_allowed_use rejects these inputs and the projection surface stays free of model output. Every value above is a verbatim printed cell with its official page linked; nothing is inferred.
