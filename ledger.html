Pre-registration & pick ledger

The brief's anti-hindsight machinery is implemented and tested: a strategy version is frozen and hashed before any pick can reference it, each pick is appended to a hash-chained ledger that cannot be edited quietly, and a settlement entry is written only from a verified final. The ledger below is empty because rights gate G0 has not cleared — an empty ledger is the correct state, not a missing feature.

!
Why there are no entries.

Sealing a pick requires originating one, and originating a line from NBA Statistics for publication is refused while G0 stands (NBA.com Terms §9; owner decision D-01 / IR-T4 outstanding). The ledger therefore records the refusal instead of a placeholder hash — a placeholder would be indistinguishable from a real entry later.

What a sealed entry will look like

A frozen strategy hash, a pick hash chained to the previous entry, the market and line as originated, the observation time, and — after verified finals — a settlement stamp naming the results file that settled it. Every field is written once; corrections are new entries, never edits.

Ordering is the point

Freeze, then pick, then hash, then settle — in that order, every time. Skipping the freeze is what the mechanism exists to prevent; skipping the settlement stamp would let an unsettled claim look final. Until G0 clears, the sequence stops at the freeze.
