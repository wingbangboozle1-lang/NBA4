Ledger & seals

Nothing in this project may be a bet until a strategy is pre-registered and frozen, and nothing may claim a win until a sealed ledger is settled from verified finals. This page documents that machinery exactly as implemented — pre-registration freeze, per-pick hashes, the day seal before tip, and the settlement stamp — and reports its live state, which is empty: rights gate G0 is uncleared, so zero picks have ever been published and the production ledger holds no entries. The mechanics are proven on synthetic fixtures in the test suite; those demo values are labelled synthetic and are not this project's record (stances D-01/IR-T4 stand).

!
The production ledger is empty by design.

With G0 uncleared there is no authorised input, therefore no pick, therefore no hash, no seal and no settlement — the honest state of the ledger is zero entries, not a demo run. Any hash or stamp shown in the test suite is generated from fabricated fixtures on synthetic.invalid and can never stand in for a published pick.

A strategy is authorised to bet only after its registration is complete and frozen before the first bet. Placeholders and silent defaults are failures, not convenience.

What a stamp does not prove

  • A seal proves a pick was published before tip — not that it was right.
  • A settlement stamp proves the finals were verified — not that the model has skill.
  • A hash chain proves nothing was edited — not that the inputs were licensed.
