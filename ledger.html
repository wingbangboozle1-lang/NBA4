Settlement ledger and pre-registration: procedure recorded, entries withheld

The brief’s tamper-evident chain has three stages: freeze the pre-registration file before the first bet; hash each pick and publish the hash before tip-off; verify the chain after settlement against official finals with a visible stamp. This page publishes the procedure and the current (empty) state. It does not perform any stage on evidence that does not exist.

Why the ledger is empty

Picks are barred at three independent gates: strategy validation (nothing has passed), rights gate G0 (NBA statistics may not feed gambling activity per Terms of Use s.1, s.9(ii)), and settlement evidence (quarter scores for the 11/15 games are not transcribed — flag P9-05 — while the 11/14 quarters remain the only full verified set). The chain design is recorded so that when a pick does exist, its pre-tip hash and post-settlement verification are mechanical.
