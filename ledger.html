NO SEALED ENTRY

Bet surface · gated

Seal before tip, verify after settlement

The brief asks for a tamper-evident ledger: hash each published pick, publish the hash before tip-off, and verify it after settlement. The mechanism is implemented and tested in engine/publishedledger.py. It is empty by construction here, because publishing a pick at all is blocked — and a ledger with no entries is the only truthful rendering of that state.

!
Why every row below reads [EMPTY].

Sealing requires a pick. Publishing a pick requires the rights gate (NBA Terms of Use §9(ii) bars use of NBA Statistics in connection with gambling) and a validated, pre-registered strategy. Neither is cleared, so no pick is published, no hash is computed from a real pick, and no settlement is stamped. The table is not “pending”; it is a specification of what would be sealed and what blocks it.

How the seal is built

  1. Per-pick hash. The pick payload is canonicalised (sorted keys, no whitespace) and hashed with SHA-256.
  2. Append-only chain. Each entry carries prev_hash and its own entry_hash = sha256(prev_hash ‖ payload); editing, deleting or reordering an earlier entry breaks every later hash.
  3. Seal at or before the earliest tip. The pick hashes are the leaves of a binary Merkle tree; the root is written as a SEAL entry, and an inclusion proof shows a published pick was inside the sealed set.
  4. Settle from verified finals. A SETTLE entry stores the settlement stamp, the hash of the official final-score observation and the outcome. Settlement is refused unless two official observations agree.

What it proves — and what it does not

Proves: integrity (nothing was edited after publication) and timing (the pick existed before the tip).

Does not prove: that the forecast was good, or that the hash existed at a time this repository does not control. A truly external timestamp — a third-party transparency log or a notary — is a separate owner decision and stays an open question on Decision questions.

Also does not prove: that the settlement source is the one a bookmaker used. That needs a market specification, which is a registered missing input (Q5).
