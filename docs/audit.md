## Immediate disposition

The useful deliverable at this stage is an evidence-led decision dashboard and a rigorous, explicitly unvalidated research/validation plan. Do **not** issue picks, fair lines, parlay probabilities, injury-minute adjustments, correlations, leaderboard awards, or performance claims until data rights, licensed sources, timestamps, evaluation thresholds, and jurisdiction/hosting choices are resolved. “Remove luck” cannot be guaranteed; validation can measure uncertainty and reduce overfitting, but it cannot eliminate random game outcomes.
