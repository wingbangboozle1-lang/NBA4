## What is deliberately not suggested

- Anything that fills a line, probability, correlation, minute estimate or pick with a value that has no official source: the brief forbids inference, so those cells stay blank until licensed data and a validated model exist.
- Anything that republishes NBA statistics values (per-game or season numbers) rather than the league's own status/schedule publications: §9(iv)–(vii) is the binding constraint, not the site's convenience.
