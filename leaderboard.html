Leaderboard & correlation values

The competition model is designed here but not operated: the leaderboard ranks only candidates that pass every pre-registered gate on a frozen strategy version, and the correlation values that combos need are measured on licensed, paired outcomes. Both tables are empty on purpose.

!
An empty board is the correct state today.

No strategy has been trained, and no production-data validation gate has passed; the gate report is blocked or not run because authorized inputs are absent. No reward terms exist. Ranking an unvalidated candidate would present luck as skill; the Netherlands Gambling Authority's consumer guidance notes that gambling outcomes are almost always determined by chance rather than insight or practice.

How the board decides

Eligibility and scoring mechanics are implemented and tested. Some ranking policy remains open; the documented tie-break proposal is not currently enforced, and no eligible entries are published.

Luck versus skill: what can be claimed

  • "Luck-free strategy" — not supportable; game outcomes remain random.
  • "Beats the market" — not supportable yet; needs a licensed benchmark, a walk-forward test and an untouched holdout.
  • "Calibrated" — not supportable yet; needs a reliability table and a proper score on out-of-sample data.
  • "No leakage" — mechanically checked by the engine for every forecast.
  • "Fresh data" — mechanically checked against the declared tolerance.

Rewards stay disabled

No score weights, minimum samples, ROI threshold, drawdown limit, prize, tie policy or payment schedule has been defined. A leaderboard is not a substitute for a valid holdout, and a rewards scheme needs jurisdiction-specific review before it exists.
