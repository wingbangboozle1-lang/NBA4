Strategy leaderboard & rewards framework

The competition shape is fixed here, before any result exists. Entries are version-locked strategies that must clear every validation gate before they can be ranked. A lucky streak cannot win: ranking requires an uncertainty interval that beats the baseline, a minimum settled sample, and a multiple-trials correction. Rewards are proposed tiers only — nothing is offered until legal review clears the reward model.

How to enter (when blockers clear)

  1. Resolve data rights (Q1) and product/jurisdiction classification (Q3).
  2. Freeze one market, one candidate strategy and the full parameter register.
  3. Preregister baselines, metric, sample/power plan, holdout window and reward terms.
  4. Run in the shadow ledger for the preregistered window.
  5. Publish only if every gate passes; otherwise show the failure reason and keep scores blank.

What this board is not

Not a promise of profit, not a ranking of luck, and not a substitute for a valid holdout. Past placement never guarantees future results, and gambling outcomes are almost always determined by chance rather than insight — a general consumer caution from the Ksa, not a basketball-specific study.
