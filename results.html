Reading the two refusals

Feed delay. The results pages print a tip time and quarter scores but no clock at the final whistle, so hours between a game’s end and our retrieval cannot be measured from the authorized documents. What can be stated is the 13.92-hour lag between the last printed tip (2026-11-17 02:00 UTC, the 9:00 PM ET tip of 2026-11-16) and the fetch (15:55 UTC), and that is labelled as lag after the tip, never as the end of the game.

Home-court advantage. The measured league home-win rate is 0.5196 (106-98 in HOME records, neutral-site games held out). Converted through the model’s own normal approximation that is +0.59 points — but engine/ratings.py still ships HOME_ADVANTAGE_POINTS = 0.0, because engine/homerate.py refuses to hand the value to a line until the run is pre-registered and rights gate G0 has cleared the standings input.
