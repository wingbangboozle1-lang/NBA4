Reading the two refusals

Feed delay. The results pages print a tip time and quarter scores but no clock at the final whistle, so hours between a game’s end and our retrieval cannot be measured from the authorized documents. What can be stated is the lag between the last printed tip of the 11/15 slate (2026-11-16 05:00 UTC for the 10:00 PM ET game) and the fetch (14:47 UTC), and that is labelled as lag after the tip, never as the end of the game.

Home-court advantage. The measured league home-win rate is now 0.5204 (102-94 in HOME records over 196 decided home games, neutral-site games held out) — down from 0.5294 a day earlier, which is exactly the kind of drift a single day of a young season produces. Converted through the model’s own normal approximation that is +0.614 points — but engine/ratings.py still ships HOME_ADVANTAGE_POINTS = 0.0, because engine/homerate.py refuses to hand the value to a line until the run is pre-registered and rights gate G0 has cleared the standings input. The rate is reported, the point value is reported as derived, and neither is applied.
