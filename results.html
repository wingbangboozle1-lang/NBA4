Reading the two refusals

Feed delay. The results pages print a tip time and quarter scores but no clock at the final whistle, so hours between a game's end and our retrieval cannot be measured from the authorized documents. What can be stated is the 9.78-hour lag between the last printed tip (2026-11-15 02:00 UTC) and the fetch (11:47 UTC), and that is labelled as lag after the tip, never as the end of the game.

Home-court advantage. The measured league home-win rate is 0.5294 (99-88 in HOME records, neutral-site games held out). Converted through the model's own normal approximation that is +0.885 points — but engine/ratings.py still ships HOME_ADVANTAGE_POINTS = 0.0, because engine/homerate.py refuses to hand the value to a line until the run is pre-registered and rights gate G0 has cleared the standings input.
