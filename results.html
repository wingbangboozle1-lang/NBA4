Reading the two refusals

Feed delay. The results pages print a tip time and quarter scores but no clock at the final whistle, so hours between a game’s end and our retrieval cannot be measured from the authorized documents. What can be stated is the 4.00-hour lag between the last printed tip (2026-11-19 03:00 UTC, the 10:00 PM ET tip of 2026-11-18) and the fetch (07:00 UTC), and that is labelled as lag after the tip, never as the end of the game.

Home-court advantage. The measured league home-win rate is 0.5023 (111-110 in HOME records over 221 home games, neutral-site games held out). Converted through the model’s own normal approximation that is +0.07 points — but engine/ratings.py still ships HOME_ADVANTAGE_POINTS = 0.0, because engine/homerate.py refuses to hand the value to a line until the run is pre-registered and rights gate G0 has cleared the standings input.
