Decision workflow

  1. Read the row and its trade-offs in the full register.
  2. Choose a path (or propose a new one) — recommendations are proposals only.
  3. Record the decision with date and initials in the register's decision log.
  4. Only then implement; re-verify affected rows afterwards.
