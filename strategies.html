Reducing luck, not promising to remove it

Use frozen predictions, future-only tests, baselines, calibration, uncertainty intervals and robustness checks to reduce hindsight and overfitting. Random game outcomes remain. The Ksa’s safe-gambling guidance makes a general chance warning; it is not a basketball-specific study.
