How the redistribution will work when the gates clear

When a player is ruled out, his minutes are redistributed explicitly — never implicitly — in two steps: the top eight rotation players by played minutes absorb shares proportional to their played-minute share; the replacement group (the next auditioned players) absorbs shares proportional to auditioned share; any remainder becomes replacement minutes. Production is scaled by the same shares. The rule is recorded here and in the engine work items; on this run it is not applied, because the played-minute shares are model inputs and gate G0 blocks NBA statistics from that use (IR-T2). The slate’s availability column shows the printed report state instead — never a projected redistribution.
