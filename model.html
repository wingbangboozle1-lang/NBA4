Two estimators · disagreement first

The model surface, and every place it refuses

The brief asks for two independent estimators with their disagreement as the headline confidence signal, home-court advantage pinned to the measured home-win rate, a total modelled as possessions × points-per-possession, luck as a z-score with a noise band, and a market-anchored prior blended by out-of-sample log loss. All of that is implemented and unit-tested in engine/. None of it is fed NBA data here, because the input right is not cleared — so this page publishes the specification, the published inputs, and the refusals, not a forecast.

!
Read this before the tables.

A model value on a page invites a reader to treat it as a prediction. Every row below therefore states either (a) a formula with its source, (b) a count taken from an official document, or (c) an explicit refusal with a code. Where a number would require a licensed input, the cell says gated rather than showing a number.

The two estimators, and the disagreement as the headline

Estimator A and estimator B consume the same authorised ledger but estimate team strength differently. The published headline is |A − B|; the average is a footnote and is never called a confidence probability.

What one game row would carry, once the gates clear

  1. Estimator A margin, estimator B margin and the absolute gap as the headline.
  2. The registered band label, or UNBANDED.
  3. Total as possessions × PPP, with the naive total printed beside it.
  4. Rest, back-to-back and 3-in-4 from the official schedule.
  5. Injury revision time and retrieval time, and the rotation availability counts.
  6. Home-court points from the measured rate, flagged derived.

And never: a price, a stake, an edge or a pick. Those are on the bet surface, behind the gate.

What blocks it, in order

  • G0 rights. NBA Terms §9(ii) bars use of NBA Statistics in connection with gambling, including legal gambling.
  • Authorised ledger. No historical game, possession or lineup feed is contracted.
  • Pre-registration. No strategy file is frozen, so no threshold or band exists.
  • Out-of-sample validation. Nothing has been scored on a held-out window.
