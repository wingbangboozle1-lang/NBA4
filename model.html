Model & estimator status

Every component the brief asks for exists in engine/ with unit tests on synthetic fixtures. None of them has been run on NBA-derived inputs: rights gate G0 blocks forecasting use of NBA Statistics, and no authorized team-strength, market or rotation dataset exists in this repository. This page is the honest inventory — what is built, where it lives, and what stays deliberately blank.

!
G0 is a hard stop, not a delay.

NBA.com Terms of Use §9 restrict NBA Statistics in connection with gambling activity, and this project's line-origination scope falls inside that restriction until an owner decision (D-01 / IR-T4) or a written rights grant clears it. Until then no spread, total, probability, edge or pick is computed for publication — refusals are recorded instead of values.

“Implemented & tested” means synthetic

Each engine module ships unit tests that run on fabricated fixtures (marked synthetic.invalid in the fixtures). Passing tests prove the mechanics — refusals fire, formulas invert, coverage gaps surface — not any claim about NBA teams. The suite currently counts 481 unit tests across eleven files.

The path to a number

A value appears on this site only after: G0 rights clearance, a point-in-time dataset (G1), pre-registration of the strategy version, and the validation gates on the strategies page — in that order. Until then the data pages publish observations (schedules, reports, finals, standings) and this page publishes refusals.
