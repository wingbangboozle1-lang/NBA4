Own lines, separate from the market

How a line is originated

An originated line is the output of this project's own model, derived only from a predictive distribution estimated on authorised data. It is never copied from or anchored to a market price. A market quote is stored separately, with its own timestamp, and the comparison between the two is a separate, later step that has its own gates.

Publish rules

  1. A line is published with its probability, model version, input snapshot hash and emission time.
  2. A forecast is immutable: a correction is a new version, never an overwrite.
  3. A pick needs a specified market, a licensed benchmark quote and every gate passed; otherwise it is a no_bet with a reason code.
  4. The stake fraction is a separate, declared policy — the engine returns 0 by design.
  5. No league-derived value enters a model without written permission.

What is verified today

The arithmetic is pinned by unit tests over synthetic fixtures: fair-price round trip, complementary cover probabilities, de-vig behaviour on a known pair, and the fact that a positively correlated leg pair has a joint probability above the product of its marginals.
